How it works

  1. Reports arrive through the form or an adapter.
  2. Scope, excluded classes, duplicates and injection are filtered.
  3. Steps are parsed and locked, then replayed in a sandbox.
  4. Deterministic verifiers decide whether the bug is proven.
  5. A human always makes the final call.

Recommendations and evidence only — DeSlop never auto-rejects a report.
