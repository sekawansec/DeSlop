Researcher guide

Every report is replayed against the program's staging environment. Prose is never accepted as proof.

The rule

Don't tell us it's a bug — show us. Your raw requests are replayed verbatim against staging, with your cookies and tokens replaced by the program's own validator accounts. A deterministic verifier then checks whether the claimed effect actually happened. If it can't be reproduced, your report goes to a human — unproven, not auto-rejected.

A good report

Steps — numbered, concrete, no filler:

1. Log in as the attacker account (session cookie below)
2. GET the victim's object: /api/profile/alice
3. Response contains Alice's private fields, not Bob's

Raw request — the exact bytes, including the Host header and the attacker's session:

GET /api/profile/alice HTTP/1.1
Host: api.example.com
Cookie: session=attacker-session-here

Screenshots and console logs help the human triager, but they are not proof: only the replayed request and its response decide the verdict.

After you submit

You get a private tracking link — keep it. It shows your report's status: received → under review → a decision, plus any questions we send you. If we ask for information, reply on that page; unanswered requests stall the report.

Rules

  • Stay in scope. Out-of-scope tests are rejected.
  • Only test with accounts you created. No access to real user data.
  • No destructive testing: no data modification, DoS, or pivoting.
  • You attest that you reproduced it yourself. False claims lead to a ban.
